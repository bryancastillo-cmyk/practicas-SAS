## 1. Topic 2 · Human Uses of Energy · Lesson 3: Renewable energy resources
Páginas del libro: 74 a 79

Subtemas:
- Is renewable energy all around?
- Renewable fuel
- Hydropower
- Energy that does not run out

Habilidades que se evalúan:
- Distinguish between renewable and nonrenewable energy sources
- Describe the advantages of renewable energy resources over nonrenewable energy resources
- Give examples of renewable energy resources
- Describe the main renewable energy resources used to provide electricity

## 2. Topic 2 · Human Uses of Energy · Lesson 4: Environmental impact of energy use
Páginas del libro: 86 a 90

Subtemas:
- Impact of energy production
- Impacts of nuclear power
- Impact of obtaining fuel
- How can the use of energy damage ecosystems?
- Impact of transporting fuels

Habilidades que se evalúan:
- Describe how the use of different natural energy resources affects the local and global environments
- Identify how technology can improve the environmental effects of using a given energy resource
- Describe the impact of energy production in the surrounding ecosystems
- Identify the impact of transporting fuels in terms of pipes, leaks, and shipping
